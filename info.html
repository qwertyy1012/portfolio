Intelligent Medication Dispenser

This is a portable device designed to help patients take their medication on time and reduce missed doses. It provides scheduled reminders, detects missed medication intake, and helps prevent accidental overdosing by restricting access to unscheduled doses. The system aims to make medication management safer, easier, and more convenient, especially for senior citizens and patients with chronic illnesses.
